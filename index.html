Questions

Do my guys need to create accounts?

No. Open the app on a phone or a shared jobsite tablet, tap "Crew View", and each person taps their name.

Does it work on phones?

Yes. The crew side is built for phones first. Add it to your home screen and it works like an app.

Where is my data stored?

On your device. You can download a backup file anytime from Settings and restore it on another device.

Can I cancel?

Anytime, in one click. You drop back to the free plan and keep your data.
